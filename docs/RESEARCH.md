# Research log

How the guide was built and how it has changed.

## Sources

- **Primary:** GBFans thread [Hasbro 1984 Proton Pack BIG Overhaul and Customisation](https://forum.gbfans.com/t/hasbro-1984-proton-pack-big-overhaul-and-customisation/219236), posts #1–91 (22 July – 2 November 2026). Indexed post by post in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
- **Context:** the GBFans wiki pages [Proton Pack](https://www.gbfans.com/wiki/equipment/proton-pack), [Proton Pack Models](https://www.gbfans.com/wiki/equipment/proton-pack/models) and [Equipment Plans](https://www.gbfans.com/wiki/equipment/plans), checked to confirm the wiki has no consumer-pack modding page, and the forum's [Proton Pack Section Index Thread](https://forum.gbfans.com/t/the-proton-pack-section-index-thread/35156) (2011), which predates the Hasbro packs.
- **Not used as sources:** vendor product pages and YouTube videos are linked where builders linked them, but the page only states what the builders reported about them.

## Why this topic

A survey of the GBFans forum on 2 November 2026 found:

- The overhaul thread was the most-viewed active thread in Equipment (about 1,000 views since July 2026; Discourse view counts restarted at the forum migration, so older threads show lower numbers).
- The first page of Proton Packs was dominated by Hasbro and Spirit questions: help with a Hasbro pack, CRT LED bulbs, resin reinforcement, a spinning HasLab cyclotron, Spirit pack mods, a charging-port mod.
- Members said they couldn't find information elsewhere (for example on removing the HGA, post #19).
- The wiki covers screen-used props and parts in depth, but not consumer packs. AJ Quick's site rebuild plan calls for a knowledge base distilled from forum expertise.

## v0.1 — first draft (2 November 2026)

- Read all five pages of the thread and indexed every post.
- Organized the content by task: before you start, accuracy differences, disassembly, exterior hardware, aluminum motherboard and ALICE frame, inner cyclotron, electronics, wand, 3D printing, wearing the pack, suppliers, open questions.
- Footnoted every claim to its post (61 footnotes, 60 posts).
- Labeled single-builder methods **reported** and methods confirmed by a second builder **corroborated**. Marked lead times and prices as time-sensitive.
- Left out: the AI print-settings workflow (post #66), off-topic content, and vendor details nobody tested.

**Known limitation:** the thread pages were read through structured summaries, not verbatim text. Figures to check most carefully:

- emitter tube size: post #12 mentions ordering 12 mm ID / 15 mm OD; post #23 says 15 mm OD didn't fit the lid and gives 14 × 12 mm;
- the Legris hole size (post #4: 8 mm bit, "think you need 9 mm");
- the motherboard hole guidance in post #68 (5/16" in the instructions);
- the BNC connector drill size (post #12: 5.5 mm, or 6 mm with glue).
